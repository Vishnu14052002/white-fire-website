Frequently Asked Questions

Get answers to common questions about our internship program

Is this a paid internship?

Yes, we offer stipends for our internship program. The amount varies based on the domain and performance level. Top performers may also receive performance bonuses.

Can I work remotely during the internship?

We offer both remote and hybrid options. However, some domains may require occasional office visits for hands-on training and team collaboration.

What kind of projects will I work on?

You'll work on real world projects ranging from web applications, mobile apps, data analysis dashboards, AI solutions, and cloud infrastructure projects.

How do I get the Letter of Recommendation?

Letters of Recommendation are provided to interns who demonstrate exceptional performance, leadership qualities, and significant contribution to projects during their internship.

Is there job placement assistance?

Absolutely! We provide job placement assistance, resume building, interview preparation, and connect you with our partner companies for job opportunities.

What is the selection ratio?

We maintain a selective process to ensure quality mentorship. Approximately 30% of applicants are selected based on technical skills, motivation, and availability.
